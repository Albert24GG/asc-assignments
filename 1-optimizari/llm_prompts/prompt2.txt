Because valgrind does not support binaries compiled for AVX512-enabled architectures, the `-march=native` flag prevents me from running `cachegrind` on these binaries. Technically, I could not use `-march=native` on the unoptimized versions, but that would possibly prohibit the use of some hardware-specific instructions. To keep the process consistent and the comparison with Haswell fair, I decided to keep the `-march=native` flag and skip the `cachegrind` analysis for this implementation altogether. Since cachegrind data is not available for this architecture, focus the analysis on execution time and infer performance differences based on architectural characteristics and known effects of the applied optimizations.
As a consequence, the `bonus/results/` directory only contains the results of the benchmarks, and not the `cachegrind` analysis. The benchmark methodology on this new architecture is the same as in the original project, with the same exact parameters.
